📌 Project Overview



This project is an end-to-end data analytics case study using the Superstore dataset to analyze business performance across sales, profit, product categories, regions, customer segments, and discount strategies.



The project combines:



SQL (MySQL) for data extraction and analysis

Tableau for data visualization

Business insights for decision-making



The goal is to identify what drives profitability and what factors reduce margins in a retail environment.



🎯 Objectives

Analyze overall sales and profitability trends

Identify top and underperforming product categories

Evaluate regional performance differences

Understand customer segment contribution

Measure the impact of discounts on profit

Identify top-performing and loss-making products

🧰 Tools Used

MySQL (SQL queries \& data analysis)

Tableau Public (dashboard creation)

Microsoft Excel / CSV (data source)

GitHub (project documentation)

📂 Dataset



The dataset contains retail transaction data with the following key fields:



Order \& Shipping Details (Order Date, Ship Date, Ship Mode)

Customer Information (Customer ID, Segment)

Product Information (Category, Sub-Category, Product Name)

Geographic Data (Region, City, State)

Financial Metrics (Sales, Profit, Discount, Quantity)

📊 SQL Analysis Summary



A total of 7 SQL queries were used to analyze business performance:



1\. Category Performance Analysis

Technology is the most profitable category

Furniture shows weak profitability due to losses in sub-categories like Tables

2\. Regional Performance

West and East regions outperform Central and South

Central region shows high sales but weak profit efficiency

3\. Customer Segment Analysis

Consumer segment drives highest sales

Corporate and Home Office are more stable and profitable

4\. Discount Impact Analysis

Discounts above \~20% significantly reduce profitability

High discounting often leads to negative profit margins

5\. Top Product Analysis

Identified top-performing and loss-making products

Certain furniture products consistently underperform

6\. Monthly Sales Trend

Sales fluctuate over time with clear seasonal patterns

7\. Overall Business Performance

Aggregated view of total sales, profit, and operational efficiency

🧠 Key Business Insights

📦 Product Strategy

Technology is the most profitable category

Furniture (especially Tables) is a consistent loss driver

💸 Pricing Strategy

Discounts above 20% reduce profitability significantly

Discount-heavy sales strategies are not sustainable

🌍 Regional Performance

West and East regions are strong performers

Central region requires pricing and discount strategy review

👥 Customer Segments

Consumer segment drives volume but lower efficiency

Corporate \& Home Office segments are more profitable

📌 Key Takeaways

Profitability is not driven by sales volume alone

Discount strategy heavily impacts overall margins

Regional and product-level inefficiencies significantly affect business performance

Data-driven decision-making can improve profitability through targeted strategy adjustments
